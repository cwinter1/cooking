## Recipe 1: White Beans & Sweet Potato Base

### Ingredients (10-12 servings)

**For the base:**
- 1 kg frozen white beans (entire bag)
- 1.2 kg sweet potato (medium-sized, 6-8 pieces)
- 1 large yellow onion
- 1 bulb fresh garlic (4-5 cloves)
- 2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
- 300 ml water
- 1 tsp sea salt
- ½ tsp black pepper

**Optional seasonings:**
- 1 tbsp soy sauce (for umami depth)
- ½ tsp garlic powder
- ¼ tsp dried thyme

---

### Equipment Needed

- Large pot (4-5 liter capacity)
- Cutting board
- Sharp knife
- Measuring spoons & cups
- Wooden spoon for stirring
- Large flat tray (for cooling)
- Food scale (for portioning)
- 10-12 small freezer bags with labels
- Sharpie or pen for labeling

---

### Step-by-Step Instructions

#### Preparation Phase (30 minutes)

1. **Wash sweet potato**
   - Rinse under cold running water
   - Scrub with vegetable brush to remove dirt
   - Pat dry with clean cloth

2. **Peel sweet potato**
   - Use vegetable peeler or sharp knife
   - Remove all skin and any dark spots
   - Rinse again after peeling

3. **Cut sweet potato**
   - Cut into 1.5cm × 1.5cm cubes (consistency is important for even cooking)
   - Keep in bowl of water to prevent browning
   - Aim for approximately 1.2 kg yielding ~1.5 liters of cubed potato

4. **Prepare onion**
   - Remove papery skin
   - Cut in half, then slice into thin half-moons
   - Finely dice into small pieces (~½ cm)
   - Measure approximately 250ml diced onion

5. **Prepare garlic**
   - Separate cloves from bulb
   - Remove papery skin
   - Mince finely using knife or garlic mincer
   - Measure approximately 1 tbsp minced garlic (5-6 cloves)

#### Cooking Phase (40 minutes)

1. **Heat oil**
   - Place large pot on stove at **medium heat**
   - Add 2 tbsp olive oil
   - Wait 1-2 minutes for oil to warm (should shimmer, not smoke)
   - Test: dip wooden spoon handle — should have small bubbles around it

2. **Sauté aromatics**
   - Add diced onion to hot oil
   - Stir frequently for 2 minutes (prevents sticking)
   - Add minced garlic
   - Continue stirring for 1-2 more minutes until fragrant
   - **Goal:** Onion should be slightly translucent, garlic should smell aromatic (not burned)

3. **Add beans and potato**
   - Carefully add entire 1 kg bag of frozen white beans (no need to thaw)
   - Stir for 1 minute to coat with oil
   - Add all cubed sweet potato
   - Stir to combine all ingredients

4. **Add liquid and seasonings**
   - Pour in 300 ml water (use measuring cup)
   - Add 1 tsp sea salt
   - Add ½ tsp black pepper
   - Optional: add 1 tbsp soy sauce for depth
   - Stir well to distribute

5. **Bring to boil**
   - Increase heat to **medium-high**
   - Wait for mixture to reach a boil (you'll see steam and bubbles breaking surface)
   - This typically takes 5-8 minutes
   - **Do not cover pot**

6. **Reduce heat and simmer**
   - Once boiling, reduce heat to **low-medium** (between low and medium)
   - Adjust so mixture is gently simmering (small bubbles occasionally break surface)
   - **NOT a rolling boil** — this dries out the base and burns beans

7. **Cook**
   - Simmer for 35-40 minutes
   - **Stir every 7-10 minutes** to prevent sticking on bottom
   - Check doneness at 35 min: sweet potato should be very soft (fork slides through easily)
   - White beans should be heated through (no frozen centers)

8. **Test for doneness**
   - Pierce sweet potato cube with fork — should break apart with zero resistance
   - Taste a few beans — should be hot and soft
   - If still firm, continue simmering for additional 5 minutes and retest

#### Cooling & Portioning Phase (20 minutes)

1. **Cool to room temperature**
   - **CRITICAL STEP:** Do NOT freeze warm food (creates ice crystals)
   - Spread entire batch on large flat tray in single layer
   - Spread for even cooling
   - Let sit at room temperature for 15-20 minutes (not in refrigerator — slow cooling = condensation)
   - **Test:** Carefully touch mixture — should be cool to touch (not warm)

2. **Portion using scale**
   - Place small freezer bag on food scale
   - Tare scale to zero (accounts for bag weight)
   - Add cooled mixture to bag until scale reads 200g
   - Remove bag, seal partially (to release trapped air)
   - Flatten bag, then seal completely
   - Repeat 10-12 times depending on total yield

3. **Label**
   - Write date on each bag (e.g., "6/14/2026")
   - Write "Beans & Sweet Potato Base — 200g"
   - Use sharpie on tape or directly on bag

4. **Freeze**
   - Place bags flat on freezer shelf
   - Stack flat bags on top of each other (saves space)
   - Freezes within 4-6 hours
   - Can be stored up to 3 months

---

### Cooking Notes & Tips

**Texture:**
- After simmering, texture should be soft but NOT mushy
- Sweet potato maintains slight structure
- Beans are fully tender
- Liquid is partially absorbed (not dry, not soupy)

**Flavor balance:**
- Salty: should taste pleasantly seasoned, not bland
- If too bland after cooking: sprinkle pinch of salt, stir, taste
- If too salty: add 50ml water and resimmer 2 minutes

**Common issues:**

| Problem | Cause | Solution |
|---------|-------|----------|
| Base too wet | Too much water | Drain liquid into strainer when cooling, return drained liquid if too dry |
| Base too dry | Too little water or burned on bottom | Add 50ml water, stir carefully from bottom |
| Sweet potato still firm | Temperature too low or not enough time | Increase heat slightly, simmer additional 5-10 minutes |
| Burns on bottom | Heat too high or stirring too infrequent | Stir every 5-7 min, reduce heat if bottom is darkening |
| Bland flavor | Not enough salt or garlic | Taste during cooking, add more if needed (can always add, can't remove) |

### Storage Instructions

**In refrigerator:**
- Keep in sealed bag
- Refrigerate at 4°C
- Lasts 4-5 days maximum
- Reheat on stovetop or microwave

**In freezer:**
- Keep in sealed freezer bags
- Store at -18°C or lower
- Lasts 3 months maximum
- No quality loss after proper cooling before freezing

**Reheating:**
- **Microwave:** 2-3 minutes in microwave-safe container
- **Stovetop:** Transfer to small pot, add splash of water if dry, heat on low-medium 5-7 minutes stirring occasionally
- **Texture after reheating:** Slightly softer than fresh, but very palatable

### Variations & Substitutions

**Swap beans:**
- Chickpeas (GI 28) — similar cooking time
- Lentils (GI 32) — reduce cooking time to 30 minutes (cook faster)
- Black beans (GI 30) — similar to white beans

**Swap sweet potato:**
- Regular potato (GI 78) — slightly higher GI, same cooking time
- Yam (GI 37) — lower GI, similar flavor
- Pumpkin (GI 75) — more watery, use less initial water

**Add vegetables:**
- 300g diced carrot (GI 35) — add same time as sweet potato
- 200g green beans (GI 15) — add in last 10 minutes of cooking
- 200g zucchini (GI 15) — add in last 5 minutes to avoid mushiness

**Flavor additions:**
- 1 tbsp soy sauce — adds umami
- ½ tsp smoked paprika — adds smokiness
- ¼ tsp cayenne — adds heat
- Fresh cilantro (stir in after cooking) — adds freshness
- Juice of 1 lemon (stir in after cooking) — adds brightness

## Recipe 2-4: Protein Cooking Options

See detailed instructions below for each protein paired with the base.

### Salmon Protocol

**Ingredients per portion:**
- 85-90g frozen salmon fillet
- 1 tsp olive oil or cooking oil
- Salt and pepper to taste
- Optional: lemon juice, soy sauce

**Equipment:**
- Small skillet or frying pan (8-inch)
- Tongs or fish spatula
- Plate

**Instructions:**

1. **Thaw protein (5 minutes)**
   - Remove salmon from freezer
   - Place in bowl of warm water (not hot — should be comfortable to touch)
   - Let sit 5 minutes — partial thaw is fine

2. **Heat pan (2 minutes)**
   - Place small skillet on stove at **medium-high heat**
   - Add 1 tsp oil
   - Wait 1-2 minutes until oil shimmers

3. **Add salmon (0 minutes)**
   - Gently place salmon in hot pan
   - Should sizzle immediately (sign pan is hot enough)
   - Do NOT move for 2 minutes

4. **First side (5 minutes)**
   - Let salmon cook undisturbed for 5 minutes
   - Look for golden-brown crust forming on bottom
   - Do not flip early

5. **Flip (1 minute)**
   - Carefully flip using tongs or fish spatula
   - Should release easily if crust has formed
   - Cook second side 5-7 minutes

6. **Check doneness (variable)**
   - Salmon should flake easily with fork
   - No translucent center (all flesh is opaque)
   - Slight resistance when forked = perfect (not dry)
   - Total cooking time: 10-12 minutes

7. **Season & serve**
   - Sprinkle pinch of salt and pepper
   - Optional: squeeze of fresh lemon
   - Serve immediately with beans & potato base

**Doneness guide:**
- Undercooked: Pink center, doesn't flake
- Perfect: Opaque throughout, flakes in large pieces, moist
- Overcooked: Completely flaked apart, dry texture

---

### White Fish Protocol

**Ingredients per portion:**
- 85-90g frozen white fish fillet (tilapia, sea bass, flounder)
- 1 tsp olive oil
- Salt and pepper to taste
- Optional: lemon juice

**Instructions:**

1. **Thaw protein (5 minutes)**
   - Same as salmon — warm water, 5 minutes

2. **Heat pan (2 minutes)**
   - Small skillet at **medium heat** (slightly lower than salmon)
   - Add 1 tsp oil
   - Wait until oil shimmers

3. **Add fish (0 minutes)**
   - Place fish in hot pan
   - Should sizzle

4. **First side (4 minutes)**
   - Let cook 4 minutes without moving
   - Looking for light golden-brown crust

5. **Flip (1 minute)**
   - Flip carefully
   - Cook second side 4-6 minutes

6. **Check doneness**
   - Fish flesh should be completely opaque
   - Should break apart with gentle fork pressure
   - No translucent areas
   - Total time: 8-10 minutes

7. **Season & serve**
   - Salt, pepper, optional lemon
   - Serve with base

**Doneness guide:**
- Undercooked: Translucent center, doesn't break apart easily
- Perfect: Opaque throughout, breaks apart, moist
- Overcooked: Flakes apart too easily, slightly dry

---

### Chicken Protocol

**Ingredients per portion:**
- 90-100g frozen chicken breast (diced or whole)
- 1 tsp olive oil
- Salt and pepper to taste
- Optional: soy sauce, garlic powder

**Instructions:**

1. **Thaw protein (5 minutes)**
   - Warm water, same as fish/salmon

2. **Dice if necessary**
   - If using whole breast, cut into 3cm cubes
   - Aim for 6-8 pieces per portion
   - Uniform size ensures even cooking

3. **Heat pan (2 minutes)**
   - Small skillet at **medium-high heat**
   - Add 1 tsp oil
   - Wait until oil shimmers

4. **Add chicken (0 minutes)**
   - Place chicken pieces in hot pan
   - Arrange in single layer (don't overcrowd)
   - Should sizzle

5. **Cook (6-8 minutes)**
   - **Don't stir immediately** — let sit 2 minutes for browning
   - After 2 minutes, stir and continue cooking
   - Keep stirring every 1-2 minutes
   - Pieces should turn golden-brown on outside

6. **Check doneness (variable)**
   - No pink color inside any piece
   - Should cut easily with fork
   - Opaque white/tan color throughout
   - Total cooking time: 12-15 minutes

7. **Season & serve**
   - Salt, pepper
   - Optional: soy sauce for umami
   - Serve with base

**Doneness guide:**
- Undercooked: Pink inside, rubbery texture
- Perfect: White throughout, tender, moist
- Overcooked: Dry, tough texture

**Temperature check (if using thermometer):**
- Insert thermometer into thickest piece
- Should read **74°C (165°F)** minimum for food safety
- Don't touch bone with thermometer

### Complete Meal Assembly

**Your complete lunch:**

1. Warm 200g beans & sweet potato base (2-3 min microwave or 5-7 min pot)
2. Cook 85-90g protein (10-15 min depending on type)
3. Transfer warm base to plate
4. Top or place cooked protein alongside
5. Optional garnish: fresh herbs, lemon, hot sauce
6. Serve immediately

**Total time:** 15-20 minutes from frozen
